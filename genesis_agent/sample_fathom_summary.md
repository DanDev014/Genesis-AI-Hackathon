# Lumen Coffee Roasters — Discovery call

**Meeting summary**
Lumen Coffee Roasters is a specialty roaster that has spent four years selling wholesale and now wants to move into direct-to-consumer. The founder, Maya Okoro, is preparing a holiday launch and wants everything live before the mid-November retail push. Their marketing lead Daniel joined for the second half.

**Key discussion points**
- Wants to reposition Lumen from a wholesale supplier to a consumer coffee brand
- Needs a full brand identity — no reusable assets today beyond an old cousin-made logo
- Launching with three blends at go-live: a light, a medium, and a decaf
- Website must support DTC sales (Shopify preferred)
- Social launch campaign to drive first sales through the holiday period

**Budget & timeline**
Budget stated at $18,000–$22,000 as the ceiling for this first push. Timeline is roughly 10 weeks, with everything live by mid-November.

**Risks & concerns**
- Tight timeline given no existing brand assets
- Founder wants to be hands-on in creative reviews, which could slow decisions
